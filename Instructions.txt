HOMEPAGE CONTACT CIRCLES

Upload cms-content.js to the root of your GitHub repository:
https://github.com/vivianongjy/vivianongjy.github.io/upload/main
Commit, wait for GitHub Pages to publish, then refresh the homepage.

The homepage contact area now displays the same contact sections and app circles as your Let's connect page.
Manage both in Pages CMS > Let's connect page > Contact sections.
Adding, hiding, or changing a circle there updates both pages after publication.
Facebook and Snapchat remain placeholders until you add their links.

This update requires connect-content.json from the previous connect-update package already installed. Do not replace it if you have edited your contacts in CMS.
Your existing content, songs, and photos are preserved.
