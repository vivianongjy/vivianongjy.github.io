SMALL TOP MUSIC BUTTON

Upload only cms-content.js from this folder to your GitHub repository:
https://github.com/vivianongjy/vivianongjy.github.io/upload/main
Commit the change, wait for GitHub Pages to publish, and refresh the website.

This replaces the bottom music section on all five pages with a small top button.
The button opens a compact Spotify or YouTube player; press play in that player to listen.
Close or Escape folds the player away while playback can continue. Reopen to pause.
Changing pages stops that page's song.
Your existing Pages CMS Song link and Music heading fields still work.
Blank song links hide the button. This update does not change your song selections.
If music fields have not yet been installed, first follow Music update instructions.txt from the previous music-update.zip, then upload this cms-content.js last.
