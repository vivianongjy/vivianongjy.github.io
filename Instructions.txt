The button reads “play me!” and appears directly above the homepage Malaysian roots / New York chapter line. On other pages it appears above the chapter eyebrow.
Clicking requests playback immediately and reveals compact provider controls. YouTube uses autoplay only after this click. Spotify uses its official embed play API. Browser/provider restrictions can still require using the native play button. Closing the box folds it away; use the provider controls to pause.
Existing Pages CMS song links and headings are unchanged. Blank song links hide the button.
