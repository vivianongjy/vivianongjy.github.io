SHOP + DARK MODE + EDITOR FIXES

Upload the seven website files from this folder to your GitHub repository root:
https://github.com/vivianongjy/vivianongjy.github.io/upload/main
Commit the changes, wait for GitHub Pages to finish publishing, then refresh.

This update does not include business-content.json, so existing products and shop
settings are preserved. If you have never installed the shop, first install the
business-section-update package, then upload this update. Never replace your live
business-content.json with the empty starter after adding products.

DARK MODE
Portrait circle captions, placeholder numbers, pop-up messages and contact labels
now follow the theme. Contact logos use a light badge for readable brand colors,
including custom uploaded logos. Switching back restores light-mode styling.

OWNER EDIT
Selected item controls now appear at the top, away from the bottom edit toolbar.
They wrap on mobile and hide while an editing dialog is open.

HOW TO ADD PRODUCTS
1. Open business.html and press Owner edit. Sign in using your existing owner key.
2. Your shop desk appears above the search bar. Open Shop settings first.
3. Select your currency. Add an inquiry email, delivery regions/cost/timing and
   your return/cancellation terms. Keep inquiries closed while preparing.
4. Press Add product. Enter its name, price (e.g. 24.50), description, collection,
   optional SKU/product reference, photo, and availability.
5. Press Apply product to preview. Repeat for more products.
6. Press Save changes in the bottom toolbar to save to GitHub. Wait for Pages
   publishing, then refresh to verify the visitor view.
7. Edit products from the shop desk list, including hidden and sold-out products.
8. When ready, open Shop settings, enable email order inquiries, Apply and Save.

A basket inquiry opens the customer's email app. It does not take payment,
reserve stock, confirm an order, or send email without the customer sending it.
Online card checkout, automated taxes/shipping and order management still require
choosing and connecting a commerce/payment provider.
